---
name: Rewards refund liability
description: Accounting policy for refunding completed portal orders after Cash Back has been earned or spent.
---

Full refunds reverse all points and Cash Back earned by the order, then restore any Cash Back that the customer applied to that order. A customer's Cash Back balance may become negative when the earned amount has already been spent.

**Why:** Capping the reversal at zero would let a customer keep rewards from a fully refunded purchase. The negative balance records the amount to recover from future earnings.

**How to apply:** Use this rule for full portal-order refunds. If partial returns are introduced, define proportional reversal behavior separately before extending this policy.
