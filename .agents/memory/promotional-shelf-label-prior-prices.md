---
name: Promotional shelf-label prior prices
description: Durable legal-pricing rule for 30-day prior-price evidence and promotional label printing.
---

Every promotional shelf label must use an established statutory prior price and preserve whether that value came from a complete recorded 30-day shelf-price timeline or from staff attestation covering a legacy period before the timeline existed. Never silently treat a migration baseline as complete historical evidence.

**Why:** Sales invoices do not prove the price offered on every day, and a newly introduced ledger cannot reconstruct earlier shelf prices. Conflating those evidence levels could make an incorrect prior price look automatically verified.

**How to apply:** Keep shelf-price events and label-print evidence append-only. Lock a campaign's established prior price while active, prevent parent promotion data from applying to differently priced variants, show provenance to staff, and block labels that could omit or clip mandatory prior/current pack and unit prices. Physical labels must be flagged for reprint whenever any legal price or promotion snapshot changes; electronic labels must refresh from live, uncached item data.
