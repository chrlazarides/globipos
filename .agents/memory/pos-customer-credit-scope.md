---
name: POS customer credit scope
description: Confirmed business requirements for wholesale POS invoicing and approved loyalty-member credit.
---

Wholesale invoicing must be optionally available from the POS and support both paid and on-account invoices.

Credit terms should be available for loyalty members as well, with a credit limit if approved.

Administrators and authorised managers can approve or change customer credit limits and payment terms.

Overdue invoices trigger a warning but do not block new on-account POS purchases within the approved credit limit.

**Why:** The user confirmed paid or on-account POS invoices, explicitly included approved loyalty-member credit, selected administrators and authorised managers as credit approvers, and chose warning-only handling for overdue accounts.

**How to apply:** Do not restrict customer credit to wholesale customers or treat loyalty membership alone as credit approval. Preserve the authorised-manager approval scope; detailed credit policies still need agreement.
