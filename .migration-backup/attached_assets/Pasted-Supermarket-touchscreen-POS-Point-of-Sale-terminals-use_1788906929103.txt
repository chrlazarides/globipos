Supermarket touchscreen POS (Point of Sale) terminals use on-screen buttons to handle high-speed retail transactions, grouped by their operational function. [1] (https://stonepos.net/what-is-a-pos-system-in-a-supermarket/), [2] (https://www.zonspay.com/blogs/industry-news/retail-pos-system-for-supermarkets-complete-guide?srsltid=AfmBOoqH1KJvT2El00Nwbs1-vBakngrrsBgg-91aHnjP4XhkPOXgSphE)Item & Transaction ControlVoid Item: Deletes the last scanned or selected product from the current active bill.Cancel / Clear Sale: Wipes the entire ongoing transaction to start over completely.Quantity (Qty) Override: Multiplies an item when a customer buys multiple identical non-barcoded products (e.g., 5 + Qty).Price Check: Looks up a product price manually without adding it to the active receipt.Item Lookup / Department Grid: Opens visual category folders (e.g., Produce, Bakery) for items missing or failing a barcode scan.Weight / Scale Input: Captures precise item weight from an integrated scale for loose fruits or vegetables.
