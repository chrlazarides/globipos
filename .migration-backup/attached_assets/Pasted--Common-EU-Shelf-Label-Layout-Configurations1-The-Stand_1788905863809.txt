 Common EU Shelf Label Layout Configurations1. The Standard Dual-Price Layout (Standard Grocery Items)This is the most common layout used for pre-packaged items like cereal, pasta, or coffee. The overall selling price dominates the visual field, while the unit price sits in a smaller, distinct font nearby. The Promotional / Discount LayoutWhen an item is on sale, EU consumer law requires transparency.
